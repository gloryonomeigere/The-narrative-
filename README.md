# The Narrative

The Narrative is a film-analysis and discussion blog focused on exploring films as works of art and storytelling.

The platform will publish articles that examine film plots, themes, symbolism, narrative structure, character motivations, endings, and the ideas a film may communicate to its audience. It will focus on film analysis rather than movie recommendations, entertainment news, awards, festivals, or behind-the-scenes content.

## Planned Features

### Users

* View published film-analysis articles
* Read individual articles
* Search for articles
* Filter articles by category
* Leave comments and discuss interpretations

### Administrator

* Log in through an admin login
* Create articles through a form
* Edit articles
* Publish and unpublish articles
* Delete articles
* Manage categories
* Moderate comments

There will be no public user registration. Only the administrator will need an account.
